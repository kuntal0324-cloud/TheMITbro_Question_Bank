# Content Production Policy

1. Questions sold by TheMITbro must be original.
2. Official syllabi and publicly stated exam patterns may guide coverage, but official question text must not be copied into commercial sets.
3. Automated validation is a gate, not a substitute for human technical review.
4. Every approved question needs a reviewed answer and detailed solution.
5. Questions with unresolved ambiguity, OCR uncertainty, diagram uncertainty or validation warnings stay out of paid papers.
6. Each released paper gets an immutable manifest containing question IDs/revisions, blueprint version and generated PDF checksum.
7. Every question must match the official syllabus for its exact exam year and paper code. A topic present in XE, XH, JEE or another GATE paper is not automatically valid for GATE EE.
8. A release must contain exactly 65 questions and 100 marks for GATE 2027 EE, including the official GA and selected-subject split.
9. Formatter status REVIEW, UNKNOWN or FAIL always blocks commercial release.
10. No paid artifact may be listed without a release manifest and successful human visual QA of the final PDF.
