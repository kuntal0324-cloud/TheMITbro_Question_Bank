# GATE EE Production Pilot 001\n\nThese are original pilot questions for validating the content workflow. They are **not yet approved for sale**.\n\n### GATE-EE-P001\n**Exam:** GATE_EE  \n**Subject:** Engineering Mathematics  \n**Topic:** Linear Algebra  \n**Difficulty:** Medium  \n**Type:** MCQ  \n**Marks:** 1\n\nFor $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$, the value of $\det(A^2-5A+6I)$ is\n\nA. 0  \nB. 1  \nC. 6  \nD. 36\n\n**Answer:** A\n\n**Detailed Solution:** The eigenvalues of $A$ are 2 and 3. For $p(\lambda)=\lambda^2-5\lambda+6=(\lambda-2)(\lambda-3)$, both eigenvalues of $p(A)$ are zero. Hence $\det(p(A))=0$.\n\n---\n\n### GATE-EE-P002\n**Exam:** GATE_EE  \n**Subject:** Electrical Engineering  \n**Topic:** Electric Circuits  \n**Difficulty:** Medium  \n**Type:** NAT  \n**Marks:** 2\n\nA 10 V ideal source supplies two resistors, 5 $\Omega$ and 20 $\Omega$, connected in parallel. The total power delivered by the source, in watts, is ______.\n\n**Answer:** 25\n\n**Detailed Solution:** $R_{eq}=5\parallel20=4\,\Omega$. Therefore $P=V^2/R_{eq}=100/4=25$ W.\n\n---\n\n### GATE-EE-P003\n**Exam:** GATE_EE  \n**Subject:** Electrical Engineering  \n**Topic:** Electrical Machines  \n**Difficulty:** Medium  \n**Type:** MCQ  \n**Marks:** 2\n\nAn ideal transformer has 1000 primary turns and 250 secondary turns. With 200 V rms applied to the primary, the secondary rms voltage is\n\nA. 25 V  \nB. 50 V  \nC. 100 V  \nD. 800 V\n\n**Answer:** B\n\n**Detailed Solution:** For an ideal transformer, $V_2/V_1=N_2/N_1=250/1000=0.25$. Thus $V_2=50$ V.\n
